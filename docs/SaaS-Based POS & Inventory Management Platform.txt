# SaaS-Based POS & Inventory Management Platform

## Project Plan and Requirement Document

## 1. Project Overview

The proposed system is a cloud-based SaaS POS and Inventory Management platform designed for retail shops, supermarkets, wholesalers, pharmacies, restaurants, electronics stores, fashion stores, and other small to medium businesses.

The platform will allow multiple businesses to register, subscribe to a package, manage their own users, products, inventory, purchases, sales, billing, customers, suppliers, reports, and integrations from a secure web-based system.

Each tenant/business will have a separate database to ensure data isolation, security, scalability, and easier backup management.

---

## 2. Main Objectives

The main objectives of the platform are:

1. Provide a complete POS and billing system for businesses.
2. Manage inventory, stock, purchases, sales, customers, and suppliers.
3. Support multi-tenant SaaS architecture with a separate database per tenant.
4. Allow subscription-based package management.
5. Provide role-based access for business owners, managers, cashiers, accountants, and staff.
6. Generate business reports and analytics.
7. Support future e-commerce integration.
8. Provide a scalable platform for multiple industries and business types.

Main responsibilities:

## 6.2 Multi-Tenant Architecture Requirement

The platform should follow a central SaaS architecture.

### Master Database Responsibilities

* Tenant registration
* Tenant subscription
* Package management
* Payment records
* Tenant database connection details
* Tenant status
* Super admin users

### Tenant Database Responsibilities

* Tenant users
* Tenant products
* Tenant inventory
* Tenant sales
* Tenant purchases
* Tenant customers
* Tenant suppliers
* Tenant reports
* Tenant settings

### Tenant Identification Methods

The system may identify tenants by:

1. Subdomain
   Example: `businessname.platform.com`

2. Custom domain
   Example: `shopdomain.com`

3. Tenant login URL
   Example: `platform.com/login/business-code`

The recommended method is subdomain-based tenant identification for a SaaS platform.

## 16. Conclusion

The SaaS-based POS and Inventory Management platform will be a scalable, secure, and subscription-based business solution for retail and wholesale businesses. With multi-tenant architecture and a separate database per tenant, the system will provide strong data isolation, better security, and easier scalability.

The first version should focus on SaaS foundation, POS, inventory, purchase, sales, customers, suppliers, reports, subscription packages, and role permissions. Advanced features such as e-commerce integration, mobile app, offline POS, and AI analytics can be added in later phases.
